## Music
I think about music quite frequently, because I listen to music basically all the time.

Some of my all time favorite artists are [[twenty one pilots]], [[Jeremy James Whitaker]], [[Sam Bowman]] and [[NF]]. All of them had their fair share of impact on my life.

I listen to a bunch of other artists outside of those four, too. My music listening habits follow a Predictable -> Unpredictable -> Predictable pattern, where the predictable phase is either twenty one pilots, jjw or Sam Bowman and the unpredictable phase consists of an assortment of different smaller artists I listen to without one "main" artist.
